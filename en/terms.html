2. Description of the service

The app calculates optimal sleep and wake times based on 90-minute sleep cycles. It reminds you in time to go to bed and wakes you with a real system alarm. Optionally it simulates a natural sunrise through Philips Hue bulbs to wake you gently at the end of a sleep cycle. The sunrise automation runs directly on the Hue Bridge and works even when your device is switched off at night.

3. Requirements

  • A current iPhone (iOS) or Android smartphone
  • Optional for the light wake-up: a Philips Hue Bridge (2nd generation) with at least one bulb. For the full colour gradient of a natural sunrise we recommend Hue Color bulbs
  • Wi-Fi connection on the same network as the Hue Bridge

The alarm and reminders also work without Philips Hue.
