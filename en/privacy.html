4. Local data on your device

The following information is stored exclusively locally on your device:

  • IP address and access token of your Hue Bridge
  • Selection of the controlled bulbs
  • Sleep plan, wake times, weekdays and time to fall asleep
  • App settings (e.g. sunrise duration and final brightness)

This data is not shared with third parties and can be removed at any time by deleting the app.

5. Hue Bridge – local communication

The app communicates with your Philips Hue Bridge exclusively over your local Wi-Fi network. No data is sent over the internet to Philips, Signify or other third parties. Communication happens directly between your device and the bridge in your home network. The sunrise is stored as an automation on the bridge itself and runs there even when your device is switched off.

6. System alarm & notifications

The alarm and the bedtime reminders are scheduled through your operating system's alarm and notification functions and run there. No data is transmitted to us or third parties for this.

7. Apple Health / Health Connect

If you allow it, the app reads your sleep data from the past night (sleep duration and phases) in retrospect to show you a review in the morning. Access is read-only, the review happens on the device, and no health data is transmitted to us or third parties. You can revoke this permission at any time in your system settings.

8. Anonymous usage statistics (TelemetryDeck)

To improve the app we use TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH). TelemetryDeck collects only anonymous signals (e.g. "app was launched"), fully avoids advertising IDs and personal identifiers, and is designed for privacy in line with GDPR. No conclusions about individual people are possible. Privacy information: telemetrydeck.com/privacy

9. In-app purchases (RevenueCat)

Premium features are unlocked via a subscription. Payment is handled by the respective store (Apple App Store or Google Play). To manage the purchase status we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). Purchase and subscription information is processed; the app receives no payment or account data. The privacy policies of RevenueCat, Apple and Google also apply.

10. Your rights

No data that directly identifies you is collected. You can remove all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the app. If you have any privacy questions, you can contact us at any time at 90minuten.schlaf@gmail.com.
